Explain the treatment. Reduce the uncertainty.

OncoVis3D is a clinic-friendly radiation oncology education workspace for demonstrating cancer treatment sites and making treatment conversations more visual, structured, and easier for patients to understand.

Clinicians can open an embedded 3D anatomy model viewer, then walk through treatment logistics, setup previews, dose explanations, expected side effects, symptom timelines, practical supportive care, and clinician note templates in a static, privacy-conscious web format.

Content sources and references

OncoVis3D content is synthesized for clinical education from public patient-education materials, disease-site treatment summaries, radiation oncology guidelines, clinician review, and institution-specific practice patterns. It is intended to support clinician-led discussion, not to function as a standalone guideline or treatment-planning system.

Clinical calibration

Dose, fractionation, organ-at-risk counseling, setup instructions, supportive care, and toxicity expectations should be adapted to physician judgment, institutional protocols, treatment plan review, and patient-specific factors.

Important: References listed identify the types of sources used to build and review the educational framework. OncoVis3D does not reproduce complete guideline text and should be updated as guidelines, institutional standards, and treatment techniques evolve.
Clinical use note: OncoVis3D is for education, shared decision support, and clinical demonstration workflows. It does not replace physician judgment, consent documentation, contouring, treatment planning, quality assurance, or institution-specific patient instructions.

Treatment explanation

Patient-facing overview for the selected disease site

What radiation is doing

Radiation uses targeted high-energy beams to treat tumor cells while limiting dose to nearby normal tissues whenever possible.

What the patient should expect

Treatment usually begins with consultation, simulation, immobilization, planning, quality assurance, then daily or near-daily treatment visits depending on the regimen.
